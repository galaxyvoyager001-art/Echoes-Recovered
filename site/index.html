Source and rights

Every recording comes from the Library of Congress National Jukebox. Each item's Rights & Access statement was checked: recordings published before 1923 are in the US public domain under the Music Modernization Act. Only issued discs recorded before 1922 were used, as a safety margin on the publication date. Credit: Library of Congress, National Jukebox.

How the restoration works

The two stereo channels are folded to mono. Clicks are detected with an autoregressive model and bridged from the samples on either side. Surface noise is reduced with a decision-directed Wiener filter, hiss above the recorded band is filtered off, and a bounded EQ evens out horn resonances. Nothing is synthesised. The project repository holds the code, the parameters for every track, and its logs.

How to listen

Start either player, then use “Switch” to jump to the other version at the same moment. The A/B file alternates identical passages. “What was removed” plays the exact difference signal, so you can check that the processing took out noise rather than music.
